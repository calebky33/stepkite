# PRODUCT.md — Stepkite: summary of the business plan (Oct 2026)

**What:** country-aware career tools. Pay calculators bring search traffic → CV builder converts → tracker brings users back.
**Markets:** UK, US, India at launch; Canada, Australia next; Germany, Spain, Mexico, Brazil later.
- UK: take-home-pay calculator (Scotland separate) + UK CV.
- US: CV builder + job-ad matching first; state calculators later (avoid fighting ADP/SmartAsset).
- India: CTC → in-hand, old vs new regime; Indian CV; ₹299 pass.

**Free:** all calculators, CV builder (3 templates/country), PDF download without account, ATS check, tracker on device, 1 job-ad match/month. Ads on calculator pages only.
**Pro Pass:** ~$7 / £6 / ₹299 for 30 days, one-time, never auto-renews. Optional ~$39/yr with clear opt-in. Unlimited matching, tailored CV versions, cover letters, premium templates, sync, no ads.

**Positioning:** local formats and pay rules; honest pricing; private by default.

**Key journeys**
1. UK grad: £28k after tax → UK CV → free download.
2. Indian engineer: 12 LPA in-hand, new vs old → share.
3. US job seeker: buys Pro to tailor CV per job ad.
4. Returning user: tracker → logs interview → checks new offer.

**Never:** fabricated testimonials, user counts, vacancies, success rates; AI-invented experience; ads in the CV builder; dark-pattern billing.

**Validation gate (week 8):** 30 strangers complete a CV, ≥ 60% download, 5 buy Pro.
